** Deadline and Scheduled

** Plain Timestamp
SCHEDULED: <2024-10-14 Mon 20:0>
[[info:org#Timestamps][org#Timestamps]]

Properties of timestamp, if no ~range-type~ is ~nil~ the end and the start are the same:

#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp
  (timestamp
   (:standard-properties
    ...
    :type active :range-type nil :raw-value "<2024-10-14 Mon 20:0>"
    :year-start 2024 :month-start 10 :day-start 14 :hour-start nil
    :minute-start nil :year-end 2024 :month-end 10 :day-end 14 :hour-end
    nil :minute-end nil))
#+END_SRC

** Plain Timestamp with Time
SCHEDULED: <2024-10-14 Mon 20:00>
[[info:org#Timestamps][org#Timestamps]]

Hour and minute have values as well:

#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp
  :type active :range-type nil :raw-value "<2024-10-14 Mon 20:00>"
  :year-start 2024 :month-start 10 :day-start 14 :hour-start 20
  :minute-start 0 :year-end 2024 :month-end 10 :day-end 14 :hour-end 20
  :minute-end 0
#+END_SRC

** Timestamp with Repeater Interval
SCHEDULED: <2024-10-15 Tue 13:23 +3h>
[[info:org#Timestamps][org#Timestamps]]

Repeater information is added:

#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp
  ... :raw-value "<2024-10-15 Tue 13:23 +3h>" ...
  :repeater-type cumulate :repeater-value 3 :repeater-unit hour
#+END_SRC
