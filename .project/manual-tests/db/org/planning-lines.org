#+TITLE: Planning timestamp 
#+STARTUP: showall

* Planning 

** Simple scheduled
SCHEDULED: <2024-11-20 Wed>

** Simple deadline
DEADLINE: <2024-12-01 Sun>

** Simple closed
CLOSED: [2024-11-21 Thu]

** All on one planning line
DEADLINE: <2024-12-01 Sun> SCHEDULED: <2024-11-20 Wed> CLOSED: [2024-11-21 Thu]

** With time
SCHEDULED: <2024-11-20 Wed 09:30>

** Time range same day
SCHEDULED: <2024-11-20 Wed 09:30-11:00>

** Date range
DEADLINE: <2024-12-01 Sun>--<2024-12-03 Tue>

** Repeater
SCHEDULED: <2024-11-20 Wed +1w>

** Diary expression
SCHEDULED: <%%(diary-float t 42)>

** Multiple same keyword
SCHEDULED: <2024-11-20 Wed> SCHEDULED: <2024-11-21 Thu>
In that case Org uses the second entry.

** Not valid

*** Multiple planning lines
SCHEDULED: <2024-11-20 Wed>
DEADLINE: <2024-12-01 Sun>
CLOSED: [2024-11-21 Thu]

*** Planning not immediately after headline
Some body text first.
SCHEDULED: <2024-11-20 Wed>

*** Looks like planning in body
This mentions DEADLINE: <2024-12-01 Sun> inside text.

*** Lowercase should probably not count
scheduled: <2024-11-20 Wed>

*** Multiple same keyword
SCHEDULED: <2024-11-20 Wed> SCHEDULED: <2024-11-21 Thu>
